# Deccara Brand Design Concept

## 1. Visual Direction

Deccara should look like a practical digital front-office assistant for service businesses: modern, clean, helpful, and easy to trust. The visual system should avoid a heavy enterprise-software feeling and avoid overly futuristic robot imagery. The brand should feel like it helps real admins, sales teams, and business owners organize customer conversations into booking, appointment, lead, reminder, and follow-up workflows.

Core visual idea:

**From scattered chats to organized bookings.**

The design should show transformation: incoming messages become structured customer cards, calendar slots, booking statuses, and follow-up reminders.

Visual keywords:

- Clean
- Reliable
- Human-friendly
- Organized
- Connected
- Fast
- Practical

Recommended graphic language:

- Chat bubbles connected to calendar cards, lead cards, and status tags.
- Simple dashboard panels showing "New Inquiry", "Booking Confirmed", "Need Follow-Up", and "Reminder Sent".
- Thin flow lines that show automation without making the design feel too technical.
- Rounded UI elements with restrained corners, around 6-8 px, for a professional SaaS feel.
- Realistic business-service scenarios: clinic appointment, travel consultation, property viewing, class booking, or spa reservation.
- Plenty of white space with clear hierarchy so non-technical business owners can understand the message quickly.

Avoid:

- Robot mascots as the main identity.
- Dark cyber-tech visuals.
- Dense technical diagrams.
- Abstract AI visuals that do not show business outcomes.
- Corporate stock photos that feel unrelated to Indonesian service businesses.

## 2. Color Palette

The palette should balance trust, clarity, and action. Blue gives professional credibility, teal adds digital freshness, and green signals booking progress or successful automation.

### Primary Palette

| Role | Name | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Primary | Deccara Blue | `#1E3A8A` | Logo, headline accents, navigation, key brand surfaces |
| Secondary | Automation Teal | `#0F766E` | Flow lines, feature highlights, section accents |
| Accent | Booking Green | `#22C55E` | CTA buttons, success states, booking confirmed badges |
| Alert Accent | Reminder Amber | `#F59E0B` | Reminder labels, urgency indicators, limited highlights |
| Text | Slate | `#334155` | Main body copy, UI labels, subheadings |
| Muted Text | Cool Gray | `#64748B` | Descriptions, helper text, secondary information |
| Surface | Soft Cloud | `#F1F5F9` | Section backgrounds, dashboard mockup surfaces |
| Base | White | `#FFFFFF` | Main background and whitespace |

### Suggested Usage Ratio

- 55 percent white and soft neutral space.
- 25 percent deep blue for brand authority.
- 12 percent teal for digital flow and feature emphasis.
- 5 percent green for CTAs and success states.
- 3 percent amber for reminders and attention markers.

### Accessibility Notes

- Use `#1E3A8A` or `#334155` for text on light backgrounds.
- Use white text on `#1E3A8A`, `#0F766E`, and `#22C55E` only when contrast remains readable.
- Keep amber as a supporting accent, not a body text color.

## 3. Typography Recommendation

Recommended primary typeface:

**Plus Jakarta Sans**

Reason: It feels modern, local-friendly for Indonesian business communication, clean enough for SaaS UI, and warmer than a generic corporate typeface.

Recommended fallback stack:

```css
font-family: "Plus Jakarta Sans", Inter, system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", sans-serif;
```

### Typography System

| Use | Typeface | Weight | Notes |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Hero headline | Plus Jakarta Sans | 700 | Clear, confident, business-first |
| Section heading | Plus Jakarta Sans | 700 | Short, benefit-led copy |
| Subheading | Plus Jakarta Sans | 500-600 | Friendly but still professional |
| Body copy | Plus Jakarta Sans or Inter | 400 | Simple, readable Indonesian copy |
| UI label | Plus Jakarta Sans | 500-600 | Good for tags, buttons, dashboard labels |
| Numeric/status text | Inter | 500-600 | Optional for dashboards and data cards |

### Copy Style

Use simple Indonesian business language. Avoid technical AI language unless it is paired with a practical benefit.

Example:

- Strong: "Balas chat lebih cepat, catat kebutuhan pelanggan, dan atur booking otomatis."
- Avoid: "Conversational AI omnichannel berbasis NLP untuk workflow automation."

## 4. Landing Page Hero Concept

### Hero Theme

**Chat Lebih Rapi, Booking Lebih Mudah**

The first viewport should immediately explain that Deccara helps service businesses turn customer chats into organized bookings, appointments, and lead data.

### Hero Layout

Left side:

- Small trust label: "Chatbot automation untuk bisnis jasa"
- H1: "Ubah chat pelanggan menjadi booking yang rapi."
- Subheadline: "Deccara membantu bisnis Anda membalas chat, mencatat kebutuhan pelanggan, mengatur jadwal, dan mengirim reminder tanpa menambah beban admin."
- Primary CTA: "Jadwalkan Demo"
- Secondary CTA: "Lihat Cara Kerjanya"
- Supporting proof row: "Cocok untuk klinik, travel umroh, properti, salon, spa, konsultan, dan bisnis appointment."

Right side:

- A clean product-style composition showing:
  - WhatsApp-like chat inquiry from a customer.
  - Deccara automation step: asks service, date, and customer details.
  - Calendar card with an available slot.
  - Booking status badge: "Appointment Confirmed".
  - Follow-up reminder card: "Reminder terkirim H-1".

### Hero Visual Treatment

The hero should look like a real workflow, not a decorative AI illustration. Use light dashboard cards, clear chat snippets, calendar mini-cards, and subtle connected flow lines. The background should stay bright with soft blue-gray surfaces and controlled teal accents.

### Suggested Hero Copy

Headline:

**Ubah chat pelanggan menjadi booking yang rapi.**

Subheadline:

Deccara membantu bisnis jasa membalas chat lebih cepat, mencatat kebutuhan pelanggan, mengatur appointment, dan melakukan follow-up secara otomatis.

CTA:

- Jadwalkan Demo
- Lihat Cara Kerjanya

Microcopy:

Tanpa sistem IT yang rumit. Cocok untuk owner, admin, dan sales.

## 5. Social Media Post Concept

### Format

Instagram carousel, 5 slides, 1080 x 1350 px.

### Campaign Theme

**"Chat masuk banyak, tapi booking masih berantakan?"**

### Objective

Educate service-business owners that slow replies, manual booking, and scattered lead data are operational problems Deccara can solve.

### Slide Structure

Slide 1 - Hook:

**Chat masuk banyak, tapi booking masih berantakan?**

Visual: Messy chat bubbles on the left turning into a clean booking card on the right.

Slide 2 - Problem:

**Pelanggan sering batal karena respons terlalu lama.**

Visual: Unanswered chat notification, admin workload indicator, customer waiting status.

Slide 3 - Problem:

**Data pelanggan tercecer di chat dan catatan manual.**

Visual: Scattered notes, lead details, and duplicated booking messages.

Slide 4 - Solution:

**Deccara bantu merapikan alur dari chat sampai follow-up.**

Visual: Chat inquiry flows into customer card, calendar slot, reminder, and follow-up status.

Slide 5 - CTA:

**Saatnya chat bisnis Anda bekerja lebih rapi.**

CTA: "Jadwalkan Demo Deccara"

Visual: Clean Deccara dashboard mockup with confirmed booking and reminder sent badges.

### Caption Direction

Banyak bisnis jasa bukan kekurangan pelanggan, tetapi kehilangan peluang karena chat lambat dibalas, booking manual, dan lead tidak tercatat rapi.

Deccara membantu bisnis mengubah chat pelanggan menjadi data, appointment, reminder, dan follow-up yang lebih mudah dikelola.

CTA: Konsultasi kebutuhan chatbot bisnis Anda bersama Deccara.

## 7. Brand Consistency Checklist

Use this checklist before publishing any Deccara visual, website section, ad, carousel, proposal, or sales material.

### Message

- Does the main message explain a business benefit, not only a software feature?
- Is Deccara positioned as a digital front-office assistant, not just a generic chatbot?
- Can a non-technical business owner understand the copy in under 5 seconds?
- Does the message connect chat to booking, appointment, lead capture, reminder, or follow-up?
- Does the CTA clearly invite a demo, consultation, or discussion of business needs?

### Visual

- Does the visual feel modern, clean, professional, and approachable?
- Are chat, calendar, booking, lead, reminder, or follow-up elements visible?
- Is the layout organized with enough white space?
- Are the colors aligned with the Deccara palette?
- Are green and amber accents used only for action, success, status, or reminders?
- Does the visual avoid robot-first imagery and overly futuristic AI styling?

### Typography

- Are headings short, benefit-led, and easy to scan?
- Is body copy readable on mobile?
- Are type weights used consistently?
- Are labels, badges, and dashboard text clear at small sizes?

### Tone

- Is the tone helpful, practical, professional, and friendly?
- Does the copy avoid unnecessary technical jargon?
- Does the communication show Deccara helping admins and sales teams instead of replacing them?
- Does the content feel relevant to Indonesian service businesses?

### Execution

- Is the hero or first slide immediately clear without needing extra explanation?
- Does the design work for both website and social crops?
- Are visual examples specific enough to feel real for clinics, travel umroh, property, salon, spa, consultants, or appointment-based businesses?
- Is the final material consistent with the idea: "from scattered chats to organized bookings"?
